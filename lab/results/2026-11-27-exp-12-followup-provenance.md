**What.** Run conditions of run `x1` of issue #22 (`plans/12-extracted-ocaml.md`).

backtracking VM that experiment #9 proved, as OCaml extracted from Rocq
and as OCaml extracted from total F\*. This entry records the toolchain,
the packages added to switch `ffi`, the regeneration of the extracted

requirements 2, 7 and 8 ask for the regeneration, the package list and
fragment membership by encoding, syntax and options. Entries 2-7 refer
here.

## Pilot and budget (step 9)

cells first. Its projections, 10 runs + 10%: short 2.7 min, large 75.0
min (16 x 1 MiB for each extracted 16 MiB cell: 351 s in all, at most
36 s per cell), catastrophic 16.7 min, cata-fuel 96.6 min. The large
series stayed under decision 4's 90 minutes, so the fallback did not
apply.

remained, and 191 minutes of series were projected. The plan's
reduction rule was applied to the large series (ranks 1-10 per source:
about 21 minutes). The plan has no reduction rule for the cata-fuel
series, so it was not run
([entry 6](2026-11-27-exp-12-followup-catastrophic.md)). The flambda
row of decision 5 was not run either (no time left); it stays a
