# Experiment #12, entry 2: VM on the regular-subset cases of `test_utf8.c`, and allocation

**What.** Criterion 1: the hand-written OCaml VM
(`lab/12-ffi-vs-native/vm/vm.ml`, about 800 lines: parser that doubles as
the subset classifier, compiler to an `int array`, backtracking
interpreter with a preallocated, growable `int array` stack) run on the
mechanically selected cases of `upstream/test/test_utf8.c`, and the
allocation check around N bare searches.

**Why.** A VM that fails Oniguruma's own tests, or that allocates per
call, would make the timing comparison meaningless.

## Provenance

As [entry 1](2026-11-19-exp-12-provenance.md). Commands (bench host,
untimed, `taskset -c 0-3,32-35`): `run.py prepare r1`, which runs
`subset/select_tests.py <repo>/upstream > tests.cases`,
`main.exe tests tests.cases`, and `main.exe alloc <engine> 1000000`
for `vm` in both switches, `stubs` and `ctypes`.

## VM semantics choices (recorded)

- Leftmost-first alternation; captures restored on backtracking (both
  start and end, as `STACK_PUSH_MEM_START` does for groups under an
  alternation or a variable repeat, `upstream/src/regexec.c:2072-2082`);
  the last iteration's capture is kept.
- Empty-loop check on unbounded loops whose body can match empty
  (`EMPTY_CHECK_START/END`: an empty iteration leaves the loop). Bounded
  repeats are unrolled without the check, as Oniguruma does for small
  bodies (`upstream/src/regcomp.c:1399-1530`); for large bodies
  Oniguruma uses `REPEAT` with the check, so the two may differ on
  empty iterations of large bounded loops (no workload or test case
  exercised this).
- `{n}?` under Ruby and Oniguruma is `(?:x{n})?`
  (`ONIG_SYN_FIXED_INTERVAL_IS_GREEDY_ONLY`); under Perl_NG it is lazy.
  `{n,m}` with n > m is out under Ruby and Oniguruma (possessive) and
  error -202 under Perl_NG. `{,n}` is out. A quantifier applied to a
  quantifier other than the lazy `?` is out. `\v` is VT except under
  Perl_NG (no `ONIG_SYN_OP2_ESC_V_VTAB`: literal `v`). `\xHH` >= 0x80
  is out (raw byte in UTF-8). Quantified anchors are out.
- `^` at the end of a non-empty subject after a final newline fails,
  as `OP_BEGIN_LINE` does (`upstream/src/regexec.c:3913-3925`).
- Retry budget: one retry per backtrack to a choice point, error -17
  when the per-start-position counter reaches the limit, which stops the
  whole search (as `CHECK_RETRY_LIMIT_IN_MATCH`,
  `upstream/src/regexec.c:1558-1563`). No match-stack limit is modelled.
- No search optimiser: every start position is tried in turn.
