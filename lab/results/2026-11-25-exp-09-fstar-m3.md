## The failure

Cell 4.15.3, `--z3seed 4` (and its replication `rep1`):

- `ProofSim.fst` exits 1: `Error 19 at ProofSim.fst(466,78-466,99)`,
  "Subtyping check failed, expected type `rep code pcB k pos stk p x0`,
  got type `rep code pc' k pos stk p x0`", "Failed to prove: `pc' ==
  pcB`", SMT solver: "unknown because canceled (rlimit=10; fuel=2;
  ifuel=1)". Query: `(ProofSim.case_star_null, 2) goal 29`, failed in
  2.49 s (2.51 s in `rep1`) with used rlimit 10.000 of 10. F\* made no
  retry (fuel and ifuel are fixed by the module's `#push-options`).
- After the error F\* stopped checking `ProofSim.fst`: the definitions
  after `case_star_null` (`case_star_nn`, `case_group`, `sim`; 184
  queries) were not sent to the solver in this cell (319 of 503
  `ProofSim` queries; 814 of 998 in all).
- `Proof.fst` then exits 0 (83 queries, all succeeded), but not as a
  check of the development: F\* warns that `cache/ProofSim.fst.checked`
  does not exist and "will recheck ProofSim.fst", rechecks it without
  sending any query, and writes no checked file for `Proof`
  (Warning 247). Its "ok" in `modules.tsv` means only that `Proof`'s
  own queries passed. The cell counts as one failed full check

## Analysis

- **Done-criterion 4 for F\*: met as stated.** Three solver versions,
  11 seeds each (the default and 1..10), every result recorded; the one
  failure is recorded with its query, version and seed.
- **Brittleness found.** One cell of 33 fails, deterministically (the
  replication fails at the same query with the same used rlimit): the
  proof as written depends on the solver. Under the baseline 5.3.0 all
  11 seeds pass with no query above 74% of its rlimit; under 4.15.3 one
  seed of 11 fails; under 4.13.3 none fails, but two queries reach 82%
  and 90%. The failing obligation is a type-index equality (`pc' ==
  pcB`) at the return of a witness in `case_star_null`, the star case
  with the empty check; the run-7 review had named `case_star_null`
  (5.11 of 10 at the default seed) among the two smallest margins.
- **What is not measured.** The Z3 behaviour of a given (version, seed)
  is deterministic here (the replication agrees), so more replications
  would not add information; more seeds would. The failing cell leaves
  `case_star_nn`, `case_group` and `sim` unchecked under that
  configuration. No fix was attempted: the plan keeps the rlimits as
  written, and a fix (an explicit assertion of `pc' == pcB`, or a local
  rlimit raise) would be a proof change for the maintainer to decide.
  The prebuilt `ulib` checked files are not rechecked under the other
  versions (only the nine project modules are).
