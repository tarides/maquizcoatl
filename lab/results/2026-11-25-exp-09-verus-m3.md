### Script incident (no verification result affected)

The first driver pass wrote no `checks.tsv` row for five cells
(4.16.0 seeds 1, 2, 4, 6 and 5.3.0 seed 2): the z3 sampler inherited
`set -e` and exited when a z3 process vanished between two `readlink`s,
so the `kill` of the sampler failed and `brittle.sh` stopped after the
check, before writing the row. The checks themselves had completed: all
five `out.json` report 135 verified, 0 errors (wall 5.11-5.38 s, 7.38 s
for 5.3.0/2). The driver was stopped, the sampler fixed (`|| true` on its
`readlink`, a failed `kill` noted in `cmd.txt` instead of stopping the
script), the five directories moved to `script-error/` (kept in
`logs.tar.xz`; their logs redacted afterwards, since the script stopped
before its own redaction step), and the driver relaunched at 18:36:24Z (`drive.log`). The first pass had
been stopped during 5.3.0 seed 6, whose check completed and wrote its row
(its result line is missing from `drive.log` only); the relaunch skips
every cell with a directory, so it ran the five missing cells, 5.3.0
seeds 7-10 and the 4.15.3 row. These five
cells are therefore recorded twice, both times passing; the rows in
`checks.tsv` are the second runs. No failure was retried.

## Analysis

- **Done-criterion 4 for Verus: met as stated.** Three solver versions
  (one supported, two through the documented opt-out), 11 seeds each,
  every result recorded; the refusal without the opt-out is recorded
  once. No failure in any cell.
- **No brittleness found in this matrix.** Under the supported 4.16.0 no
  function used more than 11.3% of its limit at any seed; the largest
  share anywhere is 34% (`case_star_nn`, unsupported 5.3.0, seed 7).
  The same star cases are the most expensive in both SMT proofs
  (`case_star_nn`, `case_star_null`, `case_chk_null` here; `case_star_null`
  goal 29 failed and `case_chk_null` goal 20 reached 91% in F\*), but
  Verus's margins are an order of magnitude wider: its default limit
  (30,000,000 units per function) is large for these goals, while F\*'s
  proof modules set `--z3rlimit 10` per query, and Verus's proof spells
  out more intermediate steps (entry 17: 1,556 proof lines against
  F\*'s 774).
- **Versions differ in cost, not in outcome.** The unsupported 5.3.0 is
  1.6 times slower than the bundled 4.16.0 (median 8.55 s against 5.30 s)
  and spreads more across seeds (`case_star_nn` from 4.5% to 34%); 4.15.3
  behaves like 4.16.0.
- **What is not measured.** The comparison of the two tools' margins is
  not like for like: F\*'s figures are per query against a per-query
  limit, Verus's per function (all its queries) against a per-query
  limit, an upper bound. The vstd library is not rechecked (only the
  crate). cvc5 (`-V cvc5`) is not installed and not tried (plan). Seeds
  are Z3's `smt.random_seed`; Verus has no option of its own for the seed.
