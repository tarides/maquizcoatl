# Experiment #6, entry 2: cases, the seven suites and experiment #9's subset

**What.** Plan steps 1-2: every test case of the seven upstream suites
extracted mechanically (4,565, matching the issue's per-suite counts),
then classified in or out of milestone 1's subset (experiment #9's
fragment: concatenation, alternation, greedy star, capture groups,
character classes; UTF-8; `ONIG_SYNTAX_ONIGURUMA`; no options; forward
search) by stack G's parser, with the first reason met for each case
outside.

**Why.** Criterion 2 asks for the number of upstream cases in the subset
and the count of cases outside it, per kind; the issue's inputs require
every suite to be extracted with its encoding, syntax, options and
direction, and the 13 `test_regset` cases to be reported as such.

## Provenance

As [entry 1](2026-11-20-exp-06-provenance.md). Commands (workstation,
untimed): `python3 -I cases/extract.py <repo>/upstream > all.tsv` (exits
non-zero unless every suite count matches), then stack G's classifier
`g classify all.tsv verdicts.tsv > in.tsv`, and the informational run
`G_EXTENDED=1 g classify all.tsv verdicts-ext.tsv > in-ext.tsv`.

## Extraction

`extract.py` parses each suite as C (string literal concatenation,
`\x`, octal and `\u` escapes, multi-line calls, `#if 0` and
`#ifdef POSIX_TEST`/`_WIN32` blocks, option and error constants
evaluated from `oniguruma.h`). Per suite: `test_utf8` macros (UTF-8,
forward); `test_back` the same macros, backward; `testc` functions,
EUC-JP; `testu` (built as `testcu`) functions, UTF-16BE; `test_options`
macros with an options argument; `test_syntax` functions, with the
global `Syntax` tracked through `main` and the helper functions expanded
at each call (77 of its 221 cases come from helpers called under
several syntaxes); `test_regset` the `X2`/`X3`/`N`/`NZERO` macros
without the file-based cases (`TEXT_PATH`), kind `rx2`, `rx3`, `rn`,
`rnzero`. For `test_utf8` the output is identical to experiment #12's
`select_tests.py` plus the two-line call at `test_utf8.c:1379` that it
skipped.

Files: `all.tsv` (382,274 bytes, sha256 `d0386ccf...ecc88f`, not
committed, reproducible), the classification
[verdicts.tsv](2026-11-20-exp-06-raw/verdicts.tsv) (one line per case:
in or out, reason), the in-subset cases
[in.tsv](2026-11-20-exp-06-raw/in.tsv) (sha256 `46c625c2...5fca`) and
the informational [in-ext.tsv](2026-11-20-exp-06-raw/in-ext.tsv).

## Analysis and open questions

- The subset is small: 152 cases, all but one from `test_utf8` (the
  other is `test_options:200`, `a|abc` with `ONIG_OPTION_NONE`), close
  to the plan's estimate of about 164. The other suites fall out as a
  whole on encoding, direction or syntax. Throughput is measured by
  looping over these cases (entry 4); the case count limits what
  agreement can show, not the speed figures.
- The first reason met is reported, so a pattern with both `(?` and `+`
  counts under whichever comes first; non-capturing and option groups
  (`(?` 725) dominate, then anchors (153), POSIX brackets and nested
  classes (77), `+` (65) and the ctype escapes `\d\w\s` (62, left out
  because their Unicode definitions need experiment #7's tables).
- Desugaring `+`, `?` and `(?:...)` would add 136 cases (288 in all), a
  cheap first step towards S2 for milestone 2.
- Two patterns are out as "invalid-utf8-in-pattern": stack G's parser,
  inherited from experiment #12, rejects them before Oniguruma's own
  validation is modelled.
