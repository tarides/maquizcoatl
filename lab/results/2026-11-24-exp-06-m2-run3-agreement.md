Verus and Isabelle/HOL, on S2 as updated in run 2 (1,243 cases, 1,184

## Semantics (criterion 1)

| Row | File | Lines (non-blank) | Against milestone 1 |

Both have the items of the other rows (`ICut`, `ILookOk`, `INegFail`,
`IBTry`, `IAtEnd`, `IIter`), the same class expressions, DEV-008's
empty check (every loop, also below the minimum, comparing the
backreferenced groups), and fold and property lookups through a
generated table module.

- **Isabelle/HOL** transcribes `lean/Spec2.lean` item for item:
  datatypes nested through `list` and `prod`, `fun` with automatic
  termination proofs, mutual `in_cset`/`in_csets`, and recursion on
  `nat` written as "if n = 0" (Code_Target_Nat), as in milestone 1. The
  step function is a `definition` built from `case` expressions. There
  is no `sorry` or `oops`. The table theory `Tables.thy` is a balanced
  decision tree split into definitions of at most 32 keys, so that no
  term is large for Isabelle's inner syntax (553 KB).
- **Verus** remains exec code verified for safety (bounds, overflow,
  termination; 53 functions verified, 0 errors), with no functional
  specification, as in milestone 1. The pattern is an arena of nodes
  with side pools. The pending items, backtracking points and captures
  are persistent lists: cons cells in three arenas, a list being the
  index of its first cell, each tail an earlier cell. A backtracking
  point therefore shares its items with the current ones, as the
  functional rows do, instead of copying a `Vec`. The table module
  `tables2.rs` is `#[verifier::external_body]` (generated decision
  trees with a trusted `ensures` on the fold length, at most 3).
- Transcription details that do not change verdicts: Verus finds the
  previous character start by scanning back from the position, where
  the other rows scan forward from 0, and it puts a new capture group
  at the front of the list rather than at the end. Lookups are by group
  number, and each group is in the list at most once.

## Analysis and open points

- Criteria 1 and 2 now hold for all seven rows on S2: stack G and six
  provers give the same verdict on every emitted case.
- Isabelle needed two fixes before it built. Comments are not allowed
  inside a term, and `last` and `ln` clash with constants (`last` is
  HOL's `List.last`), so the variables were renamed. Verus needed four
  loop invariants (loops are isolated by default). Engineering time was
  about 40 minutes of session time for both rows, generators included.
- Verus's arena lists are not a proof-friendly representation. The
  "safety only" scope of milestone 1 is unchanged, and a functional
  specification over these arenas would need a separate abstraction
  function.
