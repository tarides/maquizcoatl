## Decisions applied

| Decision | How it is applied here |
| --- | --- |
| Normalisation: raw lines and lines per 1,000 lines of project code | Both are given. Sizes are those of summary v2 (physical lines of the project's proof or source files at the commit at D). Creusot's size is the Rust tool, not the Why3 code it generates, so its per-kLOC figure is a weak datum |
| Anvil: only the Verus releases inside Anvil's active window | 49 stable Verus releases from 2025-11-19 to Anvil's last commit, 2026-10-12 (not 52). The three later releases (0.2026.10.18, 0.2026.10.26, 0.2026.11.02) are out |
| Series that span no release: reported separately, not attributed to a release | Column "tracking unreleased versions". It holds EverParse series 2026.10.11 (`6c006b0`, whose starting F\* pin is in no release), EverParse `270f5aa` and `56f47c8` (F\* master pins), and Creusot 1.9 (every Why3 pin is a development commit already in 1.9.0). They are in the yearly total, not in the per-release median and mean |
| Optional changes: not adaptation, reported separately | Column "optional". Anvil's elective move to `cargo verus` and crates.io `vstd` (`0cd9f9f` 3,478, `b1d8635` 88 after rename detection, `6096278` 12: 3,578). EverParse `270f5aa`: the 16-file root-cause fix goes beyond the 8-line workaround that F\* PR 4921 required, so 8 lines are adaptation and 166 optional |
| why3find: counted separately, as a companion tool of Why3 | Creusot's three why3find-only bumps (`948ab5f`, `12b57f8`, `eef25e4`) are outside the Why3 figure (they were already `unrelated` for Why3). With rename detection `eef25e4` is 2,488 lines, not 117,350; nothing in their diffs was classified, so no why3find adaptation figure is given. Two Creusot bumps move Why3 and why3find together (`b22069f`, `7c3d1e8`); their lines stay in the Why3 figure, since their share cannot be split |
| Renames detected | `git diff -M --numstat` on every unit of every classified row (blob-less clones of the six history projects, fetched again on 2026-11-19): [`2026-11-19-exp-13-mining-v3-renames.csv`](2026-11-19-exp-13-mining-v3-renames.csv). Five units have renames. Two change an adaptation figure: Warblre `a5a3120` (950 lines of two renamed files become 106: 1,633 to 789) and Anvil `a65d68c` (a renamed Dockerfile, 16 to 0: 2,299 to 2,283). Anvil `b1d8635` (optional, 104 to 88), `0cd9f9f` (optional or unrelated) and `aa757c8` (unrelated part) change nothing counted. Creusot `eef25e4` is why3find |
| Hand-written lines are the main figure, totals alongside | Hand-written excludes lockfiles and regenerated proof data. Warblre `a5a3120`: `flake.lock` (108) out, 681. Why3 gallery: the 358 regenerated session lines of the `compute` rename out, 157 (55 lines of OCaml API examples, 60 certain and 42 uncertain `.mlw` lines). Creusot: 23 (2 + 8 + 13 in `b22069f`, `e072997`, `7c3d1e8`; 31 with the nix build lines of `5ba3ac9`). Every other adaptation line is hand-written source |
| Vendored dependency `dependency/singular-4.3.2` dropped from the Verus list | Verus cadence 52 (summary v2 already did so). `experiments/13-upgrade-cost/releases/verus.tsv` itself is snapshot data and stays as fetched; the analysis scripts filter it |

Unchanged from summary v2: the classification, the series and spans of the
tool's re-run, and the median and mean over one value per stable release
crossed (a series' hand-written lines divided by its span, once per
release of the span).

Notes:

- **Anvil** has no per-series span: its pins name Verus commits or vstd
  versions, and it followed Verus HEAD for most of the year, which is also
  "tracking unreleased versions". The maintainer's decision for Anvil
  (divide by the releases in its active window) is applied instead of the
  separate column, so its whole 4,194 lines are divided by 49. Its first
  bump (`a65d68c`, 2,283 lines) starts from a Verus commit that was not
  dated, so it may pay for releases before the window.
- **Rocq**: both 9.0 migrations cross releases made before the window
  (Warblre 8.20, 9.0, 9.1; Linden 8.19, 8.20, 9.0), so their medians are
  high. After them: Warblre 103 (9.2) and 79 (9.3), Linden 12 (9.1) and 74
  (9.2).
- **Lean**: the 4.26 series (`e2e33d6`, 7,607 lines) is a deliberate
  migration to the new String API; its classification says a rename to
  `String.Legacy.*` would have been far smaller. If the part
  beyond that rename were judged optional, it would leave the figure
  under the "optional" decision, and lean-regex's yearly figure would fall
  to about 2,100 lines plus whatever the rename costs. Without 4.26 the
  median is unchanged and the mean over the other 9 releases is 236.
- **F\***: the median of 0 comes from the 11 releases of series 2026.07.05,
  all at 0 (EverParse followed an F\* branch). 1,932 lines tracked
  unreleased F\* versions; with them the yearly figure is 4,419. The F\*
  team maintains EverParse.
- **Why3**: the gallery spans 16.5 months of master (merge
  base 1.8.1); its share is the third agent estimate above. Creusot only
  tracked unreleased Why3 commits in the window, so it has no per-release
  figure; 23 of its 992 lines are hand-written.
- **Isabelle**: AFP-wide sweeps by Isabelle developers, not by the entry
  authors.

## Provenance

- Repository commit before this entry: `cf958d6` (branch
  `exp/13-upgrade-cost`). Inputs: the v2 classified table and the re-run's
  `*-rerun-bumps.csv` at that commit; sizes from summary v2.
- Scripts, in `experiments/13-upgrade-cost/analysis/` (Python 3, standard
  library only, run with `python3 -I`):
  - `renames.py <lab/results> <clones>`: rename pairs per classified unit;
  - `summary_v3.py <per-release-classified.csv>`: per-series table (the
    adjustments are written in the script, each with its reason);
  - `aggregate_v3.py <per-series.csv>`: per-project table.
- Clones: blob-less clones of Warblre, Linden, lean-regex, everparse,
  creusot and anvil from GitHub, made on 2026-11-19 under
  `<workdir>/e13/clones`; every unit is a commit of the window, so later
  commits on those repositories do not change the result.
- Machine: the local workstation (git and Python only); no timing.

## Limits

- Lines are not effort (no hands-on time, no failed attempts, no waiting
  for a dependency to be released).
- For EverParse and the AFP, the tool's own developers pay the cost.
- Shares are agents' estimates, not checked by a human.
- One or two projects per tool, over one year.
