# Experiment #13: upgrade cost, summary

Issue: https://github.com/tarides/maquizcoatl/issues/3. Plan:
[`plans/13-upgrade-cost.md`](../../plans/13-upgrade-cost.md). Source:
maintainer's state-of-the-art report,
https://claude.ai/artifact/8w6PVuiTsLCKpugpCowMHc (row 13 of "Experiments
before deciding").

The experiment refines the selection criterion "only actively maintained
tools qualify" into "active, and at what cost to follow". It has two
halves:

- **mining**: one year of history (2025-11-19 to 2026-11-19) of eight
  projects, measuring the lines changed to follow each tool release
  ([`2026-11-19-exp-13-mining-v3.md`](2026-11-19-exp-13-mining-v3.md),
  with the maintainer's methodology decisions);
- **builds**: the five projects of the issue built on their pinned tool
  version and on the latest release, with the hand port when needed
  ([`2026-11-19-exp-13-builds.md`](2026-11-19-exp-13-builds.md)).

The shares of lean-regex `e2e33d6` (0.99), Anvil `cc8ab6a` (0.05) and the
Why3 gallery (0.006) were estimated by Claude agents and not checked by a
human.

Erratum: see
[`2026-11-19-exp-13-correction.md`](2026-11-19-exp-13-correction.md)
(maintainer decisions of 2026-11-19: spot check waived, Why3 prover
deviation accepted).
