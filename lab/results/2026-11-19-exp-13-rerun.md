addressed: https://github.com/tarides/maquizcoatl/issues/3#issuecomment-7013400316.

The first run ([mining](2026-11-19-exp-13-mining.md),
[classification](2026-11-19-exp-13-classification.md)) restricted
EverParse to `lib/everparse/{cbor,lowparse}`, which exist only since

and at the head, and changes how opaque-only rows are attributed. This
run applies it to all eight projects. The first run's files are left as
they are (append-only); the new files carry `rerun` in their names.
