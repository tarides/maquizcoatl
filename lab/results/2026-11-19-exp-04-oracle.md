# exp-04 oracle: upstream ABI reference and harness validation

## What

Out-of-tree Release builds of upstream `f95747b` (default and
`-DENABLE_POSIX_API=ON`) installed into two prefixes, the exported
symbols classified as public or leaked internal, and the criterion 1-3
harness built and validated against upstream itself and against an
empty prefix (negative control). This is the reference every stub of
experiment #4 is diffed against (issue #7, plan
`plans/04-abi-feasibility.md`, steps 1 and 2).

## Why

Criterion 1 needs a symbol oracle derived from the real library, not
counts typed into the plan; criteria 2 and 3 need a harness whose
verdicts are known to be right before a stub is measured: all pass on
upstream, all fail on an empty prefix.

## Analysis

- The leak is larger than the issue's cross-check suggested: besides the
  8 internal data symbols, **106 internal functions** are exported
  (`onig_st_*`, `onig_node_*`, `onigenc_*` helpers, callout internals,
  `onig_compile`, ...), because upstream builds without
  `-fvisibility=hidden` or a version script. 114 leaked internals in
  all. Whether a drop-in must export them is a question for the
  mechanisms' verdicts: the stubs below show that none of the 24
  harness binaries needs any of them.
- `OnigCalloutArgs` and `struct re_pattern_buffer` (`OnigRegexType`)
  are listed as non-opaque in the issue's Inputs, but the installed
  `oniguruma.h` only forward-declares them (`upstream/src/oniguruma.h:712,768-769`);
  their definitions are in `regint.h`, which is not installed. A client
  cannot depend on their layout, so they are not in `layout.c`. The
  checked structs are `OnigCaseFoldCodeItem`, `OnigMetaCharTableType`,
  `OnigEncodingType`, `OnigSyntaxType`, `OnigCaptureTreeNode`,
  `OnigRegion` (`struct re_registers`), `OnigErrorInfo`,
  `OnigRepeatRange`, `OnigCompileInfo`, the union `OnigValue`, and in
  the POSIX build `onig_posix_regmatch_t` and `onig_posix_regex_t`.
- `onigposix.h` included alone declares its own `OnigSyntaxType` of 16
  bytes, without `meta_char_table` (`upstream/src/onigposix.h:118-127`), while the
  library's is 40 bytes. Linkage is unaffected: the copy relocation in
  `sample/posix` takes the size from the shared object
  (`R_X86_64_COPY OnigSyntaxPosixExtended`, 0x28 bytes); but `sizeof` in
  such a client is wrong. Candidate for the spec's deviation log.
- `check-stub.sh` also compares the sizes of data symbols between the
  oracle and the stub (copy relocations depend on them); this was added
  after the validation runs above and re-validated on both oracle
  prefixes (still all pass).
