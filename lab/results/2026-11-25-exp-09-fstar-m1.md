## Design choices recorded

- **Fuel** (FRAGMENT.md section 4, now filled for F\*): the semantics
  takes a `nat` fuel with `decreases fuel`, one unit per
  `compute_tree` call, as Rocq, so lemma (d) has the same shape in both
  tools; the structural measure `mu` remains available for lemmas (c)
  and (d).
- **Integers** are `nat` throughout (positions, bytes, group numbers,
  pcs); refinements appear only as `nat`. The extraction maps them to
  `Z.t` (zarith).
- **Statement**: `main_theorem_statement : prop` in `Statement.fst`,
  the same three conjuncts as `rocq/Statement.v`; M2 proves
  `main_theorem : unit -> Lemma main_theorem_statement`. No `assume`,
  `admit` or `assume val` is in the sources.
