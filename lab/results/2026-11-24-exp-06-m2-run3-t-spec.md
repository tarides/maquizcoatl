# Experiment #6, milestone 2, run 3: T_spec of the S2 semantics (Verus, Isabelle/HOL)

**What.** `T_spec` of the S2 semantics for Verus (verification plus
rustc, no extraction step) and Isabelle/HOL (session check,
`export_code` to OCaml, ocamlopt). Each step is the median of three
clean runs, with milestone 1's method. The table module is excluded:
both build against a stub of the table interface
(`verus/stub/tables2.rs`, `isabelle/stub/Tables.thy`), which is built
untimed and kept.

**Why.** Plan step 9 and work package G: the `m2-t-spec` figures for
the last two rows. Experiment #7 has no table for Verus or Isabelle, so
these figures settle nothing. The [settling
entry](2026-11-24-exp-07-verdicts-settled-m2.md) of run 2 stays the one
for experiment #7.

## Provenance

As the [run 3 throughput entry](2026-11-24-exp-06-m2-run3-throughput.md).

- Verus (`verus/verus2.sh tspec`), series `series-m2-3a.sh r3`
  (18:32-18:36 UTC, load 2.22-3.31). Timed steps:
  `verus --num-threads 4 --crate-type=lib lib2.rs` (verification of
  `spec2.rs` plus the 15-line stub), then the same with `--no-verify
  --compile -- -C opt-level=3`.
- Isabelle/HOL (`isabelle/isabelle2.sh tspec`), series
  `series-m2-3b.sh r3b` (18:37:41-18:41:15 UTC, load 1.16-4.72, no
  other thread above 5% on the timing CPUs). It is a rerun. In
  `series-m2-3a.sh`, the untimed build of the stub session E06T2 kept
  no heap. Runs 2 and 3 of `isabelle-check-base` (14.67 s and 14.51 s,
  against 8.68 s in run 1) therefore rebuilt E06T2 inside the timed
  step. `isabelle2.sh` now builds it with `-b`, untimed, and the three
  reruns are clean. The 3a figures stay in the raw data
  (`isabelle/times.tsv`). Their `isabelle-check`, `-export-code` and
  `-ocamlopt` medians (17.01, 23.93, 0.12 s) are within 0.4 s of the
  rerun's.
- Isabelle's steps, as in milestone 1: `isabelle build -c` of a session
  holding `Spec2.thy` (check), of its child session that runs
  `export_code ... in OCaml` (extraction), and `ocamlfind ocamlopt
  -package zarith -c spec.ml`. Each build includes the JVM start, the
  build manager and loading the parent heap. `isabelle-check-base`
  times that overhead with an empty theory.

## Analysis

- Verus's and Isabelle's S2 semantics build 1.3x and 1.2x slower than
  milestone 1's. That is the smallest growth of the six provers (Lean
  1.8x, Rocq 2.6x, Why3 3.2x, F\* 6.2x), although the files grew 2.1x
  (Verus) and 2.0x (Isabelle) in lines. Both measurements are dominated
  by fixed costs: Verus's process start and vstd loading (about 1 s of
  each step), and Isabelle's JVM, build manager and heap loading.
- Experiment #7 has no Verus or Isabelle table target, so no verdict
  depends on these figures. On a common basis, Isabelle's net 11 s
  would be above F\*'s 7.9 s (the largest of the other provers) and
  below every one of experiment #7's `T_table` (12-128 s). That is informational only.
