## How the semantics is run

Verus does not execute `spec fn`s (and `by (compute)` over these
definitions would be experiment #6's slow interpreter question, not
this one's). Each spec fn the driver needs has an exec mirror on exec
data (`usize`, `Vec`, `Box`; instructions borrow their literal from the
pattern), whose `ensures` states that its result's view equals the
spec fn's result on the views of its arguments:

| exec | proved equal to |
|---|---|
| `sem::ecompute_tree`, `efirst_match`, `espans`, `esem` | `compute_tree`, `first_match`, `spans`, `sem` |
| `compile::enullable`, `eclen`, `enchecks`, `ecomp`, `ecompile` | `nullable`, `clen`, `nchecks`, `comp`, `compile` (when no `usize` overflow; `None` otherwise) |
| `vm::emget`, `epop`, `eempty_start`, `estep`, `eregion`, `evm` | `mget`, `pop`, `empty_start`, `step`, `region`, `vm` |
| `sem::esearch_sem`, `vm::esearch_vm` | `search` of `sem` and `vm` |

So the verdicts printed for `vm` and `sem` are those of the spec fns
`vm (compile r)` and `sem r`, which `statement.rs` is about. These are
refinement proofs of the exec code, not lemmas of the theorem: the
theorem (lemmas (a)-(e)) is M2's. The exec VM is a loop over a `Vec`
stack (top last) and a `Vec` of capture writes (latest last), as the
plan's "exec Rust with `Vec`, proved against spec fns". `evm` and
`estep` require `code.len() < usize::MAX - 2` (so that `pc + 2` does not
overflow) and `esearch_*` require `s.len() < usize::MAX`; the driver,
which is unverified glue, calls them on small cases.

No `assume`, `admit`, `external_body`, `external`,
`exec_allows_no_decreases_clause` or `no_verify` is in `verus/*.rs`
(`scan.txt`); no rlimit was raised.

## Design choices recorded (FRAGMENT.md, sections 4, 7 and 9)

- **Fuel**, as Rocq and F\*: `nat` fuel, `decreases fuel`, one unit per
  `compute_tree` call; the structural measure `mu` remains for lemmas
  (c) and (d).
- **Lists** are `Seq`s with the head last (push is cons), so the
  stack and the action list map to `Vec` push/pop in the exec code;
  `skipn`/`is_prefix` became `is_prefix_at` on `subrange`; `spans` and
  `region` are `Seq::new`. These are representational: the verdicts are
  identical to Rocq's and F\*'s.
- **Two layers** (spec and exec, with views) are what Verus needs to
  run anything; experiment #6's `spec2.rs` had exec code only, with no
  functional specification.
- **Statement**: `main_theorem_statement()` in `statement.rs`, the same
  three conjuncts as Rocq's and F\*'s, over the spec fns. Its outer
  trigger (`sem` at fuel 0) only names a term with `r`, `s` and `p`;
  M2's proof does not depend on it.
