## Reading notes

- **Creusot.** Every pinned Why3 commit maps to release 1.9.0, including
  the pin at the start of the window, so no row reaches a new release:
  the whole 118,591 lines are unattributed and Creusot has no per-release
  figure. `eef25e4` ("tests: Fix why3find setup", 8,358 files, 117,350
  lines) is a test-tree rewrite triggered by a why3find bump and dominates
  the total; it needs classification before any Creusot figure is used.
- **Anvil.** The pins at the start of each bump are Verus commits or
  vstd paths (opaque), so `releases_spanned` is empty for every row and
  Anvil has no per-release figure. Keyword rows (unpinned periods
  2026-04-16..2026-07-22 and 2026-07-28..2026-11-19) are reported apart
  from pinned rows, as the plan requires.
- **EverParse.** Almost every bump changes 0 lines under
  `lib/everparse/cbor` and `lib/everparse/lowparse`. Two pins (`56f47c8`,
  `270f5aa`) are F\* commits contained in no release (`unreleased`).
- **Linden 9.2.** The per-release group of series 9.2 contains
  `31aa384` (to 9.2.0, 47 lines), `117d534` (adds 9.1.1 to the CI matrix
  and lowers the opam bound, 30 lines) and `b6396cb` (2 lines, no release
  reached). The tool groups by the newest series in the "to" set, which is
  still 9.2, so the two later rows count towards 9.2 (79 lines) instead
  of being unattributed. To settle during classification.
- **AFP authors.** Release trees are forked before the Isabelle release
  date, so authors (from `mirror-afp-devel` between release dates) and
  diffs can be misaligned by one release period. Affected:
  `Formula_Derivatives` (authors haftmann and nipkow listed on the empty
  2025-1..2025-2 diff, no author on the non-empty 2025-2..2026 diff).
- **Why3 gallery.** One snapshot of 735 files (+48,508 -34,341) that
  mixes new examples, proof sessions and adapted `.mlw` files. It is only
  indicative until decomposed; Part A's replay of the 1.8.2 sessions with
  1.9.0 is the Why3 datum.

## What Part C cannot measure

- Diff size is not effort: no hands-on time, no failed attempts, no time
  spent waiting for a dependency (mathlib, rocq-stdlib) to be released.
- A project that skips releases shows one larger diff, normalised by the
  number of releases spanned but not equal to the per-release cost
  (lean-regex skipped v4.30.0 and v4.33.0; Warblre and Linden span three
  releases with the Rocq 9.0 migration).
- For EverParse and the AFP, the cost is paid by the tool's own
  developers (EverParse is maintained by the F\* team; AFP entries are
  adapted by the Isabelle developers).
- Message patterns can miss adaptations folded into feature commits, and
  catch unrelated commits; pattern-selected periods are reported apart
  from pinned periods.
