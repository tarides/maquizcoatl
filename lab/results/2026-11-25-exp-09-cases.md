# Experiment #9, entry 2: the fragment's cases and the C oracle (run 1)

**What.** The upstream test cases of the fragment (literals,
concatenation, `|`, greedy `*`, plain groups; default syntax and
options; no case folding; no multibyte literal), selected from
experiment #6's in-subset cases and re-checked against the full
extraction of the seven suites; experiment #2's loop probes as extra
cases; the C library's verdicts on all of them, by `onig_search` and by
`onig_match` at every start position.

**Why.** Done-criterion 2 (issue #4) asks for the count of the
fragment's cases and for agreement with the C library, cases at the
retry limit excluded; plan step 1 and planned entry 2.

All 50 are from `test_utf8` but one (`test_options:200`, `a|abc`). The
three `e` cases are `*`, `|*` and `abc|?` (`test_utf8:1784`, `1785`,
`1791`).

Re-check over the full extraction: an independent recogniser of
fragment syntax (in `select.py`, written for this purpose, not stack G's
parser) accepts exactly the 50 cases under the fragment's context
(UTF-8, `ONIG_SYNTAX_ONIGURUMA`, no options, forward) and builds the
same AST and group count for each; no case outside experiment #6's
subset belongs to the fragment. Its only other in-context hits are the
quantifiers with no target `*` and `|*` (the `e` cases above).
Fragment syntax outside the context, informational (byte-level for the
EUC-JP and UTF-16 suites): UTF-16BE 67, backward search 47, EUC-JP 44,
options 20, other syntaxes 10 (GREP 4, PERL 2, POSIX_BASIC 2, JAVA 1,
EMACS 1). Full table: [counts.md](../09-representative-proof/cases/counts.md).

What the 50 cases exercise: 12 have a star (none over a nullable body,
as the plan's risk table expected), 15 an alternation, 16 a group
(`OP_MEM_START_PUSH` in 3: `(a|b)*`, `(abc)*`, `(a)|(a)`; the other
groups, `(|a)` included, use `OP_MEM_START`), and 3 are the empty
pattern.

## Analysis and open questions

- The fragment is small (50 cases) and shallow: no upstream case has a
  star over a nullable body, so the empty check is exercised only by the
  4 extra probes (14 verdicts with the start positions). Done-criterion
  2's comparison is defined but weak on loops; the theorem, not the
  cases, carries the star case.
- Checking every start position (`onig_match`, 201 + 10 verdicts) gives
  4x more comparisons than the search alone and tests the VM at the
  granularity of the theorem.
